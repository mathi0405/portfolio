01 · MOTIVATION

Technology that widens who gets heard.

Millions of people communicate primarily through sign language, yet most digital systems can't understand a single gesture. Recognizing isolated signs from video is the foundational step toward real-time translation between signers and non-signers.

This work — my bachelor's thesis — built and evaluated a deep learning pipeline for that step, and was published in IEEE in 2023.

03 · OUTCOME & LINEAGE

Where the perception thread started.

The thesis was published in IEEE in 2023 — my first peer-reviewed work, and the start of a through-line: teaching machines to see.

A companion case study — A Framework for Keypoint Neural Invariant Feature Transform (PyTorch, MATLAB) — pushed the same thread toward robust keypoint detection and matching under varying illumination and perspective.

That perception foundation now feeds the vision stacks in my autonomous-driving and Swarm-SAR work — from recognizing a hand sign to recognizing a victim in smoke.
